About

The APEC Alliance for Supply Chain Connectivity (A2C2) Regional Roundtable will bring together government officials, private sector stakeholders, and other experts to explore the role of supply chain traceability and track-and-trace systems in advancing customs efficiency and supply chain resilience. Through a series of sessions and interactive discussions, participants will examine how different industries approach supply chain traceability and visibility, including shared challenges and sector-specific considerations.
